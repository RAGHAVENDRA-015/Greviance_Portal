# Departments Overview

This document describes the **municipal departments** that handle citizen grievances within the AI‑Powered Citizen Grievance Portal. Each department is mapped to one or more complaint categories, responsible for field inspection, remediation, and status updates.

---

## Department List
| Department | Primary Responsibility | Associated Complaint Categories |
|------------|------------------------|--------------------------------|
| **Public Works Department (PWD)** | Road construction, maintenance, and infrastructure repairs. | Roads, Drainage, Public Safety (e.g., damaged signboards) |
| **Water Board** | Water supply, pipe maintenance, leak detection, and quality monitoring. | Water Supply |
| **Electricity Board** | Power distribution, street lighting, and transformer maintenance. | Electricity |
| **Sanitation Department** | Waste collection, garbage processing, and street cleaning. | Garbage |
| **Health Department** | Public health hazards, vector breeding sites, and sanitation of public toilets. | Health |
| **Police / Public Safety** | Safety inspections, traffic signal maintenance, hazardous structures. | Public Safety |
| **Municipal Administration** | General governance, inter‑department coordination, and oversight of miscellaneous issues. | Other, Corruption |
| **Anti‑Corruption Cell** | Investigates allegations of bribery, misuse of authority, and fraudulent services. | Corruption |

---

## Department Responsibilities
1. **Intake & Triage** – Receive complaints routed to the department based on AI classification.
2. **Field Inspection** – Dispatch crews or officers to verify the issue on‑site.
3. **Remediation** – Perform repairs, clean‑ups, or corrective actions.
4. **Status Updates** – Update the complaint status (`In Progress`, `Resolved`, `Rejected`) and provide **resolution notes**.
5. **Reporting** – Contribute data to the admin dashboard for performance metrics (average resolution time, pending volume, etc.).

---

## Service Level Expectations
| Department | Target Resolution Time (Average) |
|------------|----------------------------------|
| Public Works | 7 – 14 business days |
| Water Board | 3 – 7 business days |
| Electricity Board | 2 – 5 business days |
| Sanitation | 2 – 5 business days |
| Health | 5 – 10 business days |
| Police / Public Safety | 1 – 3 business days (high‑priority safety issues) |
| Municipal Administration | 10 – 20 business days |
| Anti‑Corruption Cell | 15 – 30 business days (investigative) |

---

*The department mapping is used by the AI classification service to automatically route complaints to the appropriate team.*
